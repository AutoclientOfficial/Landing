The Real Problem

Procurement shouldn't be the hardest part of patient care.

Most institutions don't switch suppliers because of price. They switch because of everything that happens after the order is placed.

Stockouts in the middle of a quarter

A supplier confirms an order, then goes quiet. Your pharmacy substitutes, patients wait, and your staff spends the week chasing follow-ups instead of doing their jobs.

Documents that arrive incomplete

Accreditation and reimbursement stall because a certificate of product registration, batch analysis, or licence copy was never submitted — and nobody told you until it was late.

Delivery windows nobody commits to

"Next week" turns into next month. Without a firm schedule you can't plan inventory, and you end up carrying buffer stock you shouldn't have to pay for.

No one accountable for your account

You re-explain your requirements to a different person every time. Nobody knows your formulary, your volumes, or the last issue you raised.
